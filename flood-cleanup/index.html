A sump pump that quits, a sewer that backs up, a pipe that lets go, rising river water, storm surge or a hard rain can each flood a property, and the damage should be handled by professionals without delay. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Green Bay Restoration handles emergency flood cleanup across Green Bay and answers 24/7 to pump out the water, sanitize what it touched and restore your property safely.

The first job is getting the water out quickly, and our submersible and truck-mounted pumps are able to clear thousands of gallons every hour. With the standing water gone, our IICRC-certified technicians check the contamination level and apply the right sanitizing steps, including antimicrobial treatment, HEPA air filtration and disinfection of every affected surface. Signs It Is Time for Flood Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through
  • Heavy rainfall, a dead sump pump and water spreading across the basement
  • Any amount of water sitting in a room, garage or basement
  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
  • Soaked landscaping that leaves water pooling against your foundation
  • Cracked foundation walls, low doorways or window wells letting water inside

Why Professional Flood Cleanup Matters

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. A trained cleanup team knows how to set containment, wear PPE (personal protective equipment) correctly and sanitize by protocol, which protects your household from hepatitis, E. coli and other diseases carried in water.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Install a battery backup sump pump so you stay protected when storms knock out power
  • Store valuables and important papers off the basement floor in waterproof containers
  • Keep gutters and storm drains clear of debris so water can flow properly
  • Check that grading on all sides of the house carries water away from the foundation
  • Roof runoff should leave the downspout far enough out that it cannot soak the foundation
  • Look into a flood insurance policy, including when your home sits outside a mapped flood zone
