Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Pathogens in raw sewage include salmonella, giardia, hepatitis and E. coli, along with other viruses, parasites and bacteria, and contact with anything contaminated is a serious risk to health. Green Bay Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Green Bay, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

What a Professional Brings to Sewage Cleanup

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

Your Sewage Cleanup Questions Answered

Standard homeowner's insurance often does NOT cover sewage backup unless you have a specific sewage or water backup endorsement. We strongly recommend adding this coverage, which is typically inexpensive ($50 to $100 per year) but provides critical protection. Check your policy or ask your agent.
Keep people and pets out of the affected area. Do not flush, run faucets or start the washer or dishwasher, because added water feeds the backup. Switching off the heating and cooling and the affected circuits is wise, provided you stay clear of the contaminated water while doing it. Skip the mop and bucket, scrub any exposed skin and wait for a trained crew.
It depends on the material. Hard, non-porous items such as metal, glass, sealed tile and solid plastic can usually be cleaned and disinfected. Porous materials that contacted sewage, including carpet, padding, drywall, insulation, mattresses and upholstered furniture, have to be discarded. For insurance purposes, our crew keeps a list of everything that had to be removed.
Most backups start with a blockage in the home's sewer line. Grease buildup, wipes and other items that should not be flushed, and tree roots growing into the pipe are the usual causes. Older lines can also crack or collapse, and heavy rain can overload the municipal sewer and push sewage back through floor drains. To know for certain, have the line camera-inspected once cleanup is finished.
In Green Bay, a sewage cleanup usually runs $2,000 to $10,000 or more, and the total reflects how large the affected area is and how far contamination spread. A small backup that stayed contained might be $1,500 to $3,000, but widespread sewage flooding can pass $15,000 once replacement materials and reconstruction are counted.

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Green Bay

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • Wet spots on floors or walls accompanied by foul odors

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape
  • Do not wait on a slow drain, which can turn into a full blockage
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris
  • Schedule a camera inspection of the sewer line every 2 to 3 years so trouble is found early
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
