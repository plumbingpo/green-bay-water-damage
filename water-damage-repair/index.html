Our Water Damage Repair Process

We follow a proven process to ensure every job is completed to the highest standards. Here's how we handle water damage repair from start to finish:

Verify Dryness First

We take final moisture readings on framing, subfloor and remaining drywall. Only when each material reads normal does rebuilding start, which keeps damp spots from being sealed inside.

Each room is itemized in a document showing replacements, the materials chosen and how long we expect it to take. If a claim is open, we line the scope up with the adjuster.

Water Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • A water stain should stay unpainted and unpatched while its cause is still active.
  • Caulk around tubs, showers and sinks yearly so water stays out of the wall.
  • Keep leftover flooring, tile and paint details so future repairs can be matched.
  • Rebuilt bathroom walls last longer when the exhaust fan runs through and after each shower.
  • Photograph damaged materials before they are removed in case your insurer asks later.
  • Use moisture resistant drywall and backer board when rebuilding bathrooms, kitchens and laundry rooms.

When to Call for Water Damage Repair in Green Bay

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • At floor level, cabinet panels and bases show swelling, peeling or soft spots.
  • Wood floor boards stayed raised, cupped or crowned even once the moisture was gone.
  • Interior doors now stick or will not latch after swelling from moisture.
  • During drying, sections of wallboard came out and the framing is still exposed.
  • Water under the tile left cracked grout lines or tiles that rock when stepped on.
  • Door casings and baseboards have warped or pulled off the wall surface.
  • Where water ran down a wall, the paint has blistered, peeled or turned brown.
